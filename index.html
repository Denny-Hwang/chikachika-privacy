Chika Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Applies to Chika Buddy 1.7.0

Summary: Chika Buddy has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases or third-party analytics. Your family's profiles, brushing records and photos are stored on your device. They are not sent to our servers. Camera images are processed on the device and are never stored or uploaded.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the app stores the following in its local storage on your device:

  • Profiles: names or nicknames you enter, optional age, chosen character, and an optional profile photo.
  • Brushing records: up to 90 days of sessions, including time, measured and planned duration, completion, guide steps, estimated coverage of four mouth areas, a detected-brushing ratio and whether a grown-up checked the session.
  • Progress and rewards: streaks, milestones, stars and their history, the Good Habit Tree's growth and decorations, and badges.
  • Chika World place: the country, or region within it, that a profile picks for its tree on the in-app map.
  • Settings: language, music and voice settings, reminders, text size and similar preferences.
  • Photos: optional finish-session selfies are kept in the app's local cache until you save or share them.

Deleting a profile removes its records. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the app's local storage.

2. Camera and Face Data

Mirror Mode uses the front camera for a live preview. On the device, Google ML Kit face detection and, on iOS, Apple's Vision hand-pose detection read the position of the face, lips and hands in each frame. These signals are used only to place AR headwear (such as a crown or hat) above the head and to estimate brushing activity.

  • Camera frames, face and hand landmarks and coordinates are processed in real time and then discarded. They are never stored or uploaded.
  • Session records keep only summary values (estimated coverage and detection ratio), not camera data.
  • The app does not perform facial recognition or identification and creates no face templates or faceprints.
  • Camera access is optional and requires your permission. Basic Mode works without the camera. The app does not record audio.

Brushing detection and coverage are estimates for habit tracking, not a dental assessment.

3. Photos, Sharing and Backups

Choosing a profile photo and saving a certificate or selfie use the standard iOS permissions; saving asks only for permission to add photos to your library. Photos, certificates, tree pictures and weekly cards are shared only when you choose to share them through the system share sheet. The service you send them to applies its own policy.

Backup & restore (behind a parental gate) creates a file containing profiles (including Chika World places), brushing records, stars, tree and badge progress, redeemed promo codes, settings and profile photos, and opens the share sheet so you choose where it goes. The file is not encrypted; please delete it when you no longer need it. Restoring reads the file on the device; nothing is sent to us.

Earlier versions (1.6.0 to 1.6.2) could also create a 90-day brushing PDF report, which was shared only when you chose to. Version 1.7.0 removed it.

4. Chika World Map

Chika World shows only your own family's trees on an illustrated map that is built into the app. It uses no map service, no location permission and no device location. No other families appear, and your choices are not sent anywhere.

5. Network Requests

  • Version check: the app sends its bundle identifier to Apple's iTunes Lookup service to see whether an update is available.
  • Review prompt: after some milestones the app may ask Apple's system to show a rating prompt; Apple decides whether it appears.

These requests do not include your family's profiles or records. As with any internet connection, network providers can see ordinary connection data such as an IP address. Friend invites and anonymous diagnostics exist in the code but are switched off in the App Store version; they would only be turned on after this policy is updated.

6. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts, email addresses or phone numbers
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers, ads or tracking
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No sale, rental or trade of any data

7. Children's Privacy

Chika Buddy is made for families with children. The app does not create accounts, does not profile children for advertising, and does not send their names, photos or brushing records to our servers. Parent-only features, such as backup and restore, are behind a parental gate.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete a profile in the app, or delete all Chika Buddy data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. Photos, cards or backup files that you already saved or shared to another app or your photo library must be deleted there. Device backups (such as iCloud Backup) follow your system settings.

9. Security

Data in the app is protected by your device's built-in security, including device encryption.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page, and its "Last updated" date, before any change in how the app handles data, including before any optional network feature is turned on.
